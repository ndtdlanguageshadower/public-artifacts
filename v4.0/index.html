NDTD Language Shadower

v4.0 · Language shadowing player and practice tool for English learning.

NDTD Language Shadower helps you practice English by listening to audio or video and repeating what you hear. The app is designed for personal language learning: you can use your own content, and it also gives you many ways to create your own practice materials. It also lets you evaluate your speaking without needing a teacher.
